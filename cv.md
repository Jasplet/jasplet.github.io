# Curriculum Vitae

I'm a quantitative researcher with experience in systematic investment management and a background in geophysics. My work spans statistical modelling, time-series analysis and scientific software development, with particular experience in commodity futures markets and applied research for the energy industry.

A copy of my CV is [available to download](/assets/files/Joseph_Asplet_CV.pdf). For a more complete record of my scientific research, publications and academic activities, see my [academic CV](/academic_cv/).

## Skills

**Programming:** Python (object-oriented), C#, SQL, NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, Polars, marimo.

**Quantitative Methods:** Time series analysis, market impact modelling, numerical modelling, statistical modelling, inverse problems.

**Infrastructure:** Git/GitHub, AWS (EC2, Lambda, S3), automated testing, CI (GitHub Actions), Docker, HPC.

## Professional Experience
